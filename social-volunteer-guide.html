Send us your photos and videos

Got great shots from a KCAA game, clinic or event? Email them to us and they could be featured on our Instagram. It takes two minutes.

How to send

  1. Email social@kcaa.caAttach your best 3–10 photos or short videos.
  2. Put the event and date in the subjectLike this: Learn to Skate – Nov 8 or Hockey game vs. Scarborough – Oct 25
  3. Add one or two lines about what happenedFor example: “Great turnout at the Saturday session — 20 kids learned crossovers.” The more we know, the better the caption.
  4. Big videos? Send a Google Drive linkUpload the video to Google Drive, share it with social@kcaa.ca, and paste the link in your email. (iPhone Mail Drop links and other download links won’t work.)

Example email

Tosocial@kcaa.ca
SubjectLearn to Skate – Nov 8
MessageGreat turnout at the Saturday session — 20 kids learned crossovers. Photos attached, taken by me.

Please keep in mind

Do
  • Send action shots from real KCAA events — mid-drill and mid-play beat posed lineups.
  • Send only photos and videos you took yourself, or say who did.
  • One event per email.
Please don’t
  • Include children’s names in your message.
  • Send photos from events that aren’t KCAA’s, or other people’s photos you don’t have permission to share.
  • Expect a reply — this inbox isn’t monitored for questions. For anything else, contact the board.

What happens next

A KCAA board member reviews every post before it goes live, so nothing is published automatically from your email alone. Not every submission will be posted, and we may edit the caption.

Photo release: families agree to KCAA photo and video use when they register. By emailing us photos, you confirm they’re from KCAA events and that you’re happy for KCAA to use them.

Saturday sessions

Got great
photos?

Send your shots from Learn 2 Skate and Hockey — they could be featured on KCAA’s Instagram.

KCAA 세션에서 찍은 사진과 영상을 보내 주세요. 인스타그램에 소개될 수 있습니다.

  1. 1
    Scan the code
    It opens an email already addressed to us — or write to social@kcaa.ca.
    QR 코드를 스캔하거나 social@kcaa.ca로 보내세요.
  2. 2
    Add the event and date
    In the subject, like “Learn 2 Skate – Nov 8.”
    제목에 행사 이름과 날짜를 적어 주세요.
  3. 3
    Attach and send
    Add a line about what happened. Big video? Share a Google Drive link.
    사진을 첨부하고 한두 줄로 설명해 주세요.
  • Please don’t include children’s names in your message.
  • Only send photos and videos you took yourself.
  • A KCAA board member reviews every post before it goes up, so we can’t post everything.
Thank you for helping tell KCAA’s story.
By sending photos you confirm they’re from KCAA events and that KCAA may use them.
